1. The candidate

I am a Supply Chain Management student at the University of South Florida (USF) combining four years of founder-level small business operations with high-volume inventory replenishment and event logistics.

At Teeny Theenys Boutique, I directed domestic and global procurement of specialty fabrics, managed nearly 200 apparel SKUs, and maintained a 100% on-time fulfillment rate across 3-week custom lead times with zero order slippage.

My background bridges hands-on warehouse cycle counting and strict First-In First-Out (FIFO) par-level enforcement with formal quantitative training in supply network modeling, linear optimization, and enterprise resource planning. I am actively seeking an internship to apply rigorous vendor oversight and operational stamina to an impactful supply chain team.
